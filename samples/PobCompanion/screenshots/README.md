The trade examples use the same Martial Artist build and real historical Standard listings on GGG's official site. The ranking capture evaluated all 20 loaded candidates, with none skipped. Twelve candidates have effects the pinned PoB engine does not model; their rows show that warning and their comparisons identify the affected text. PoB-recognized display-only modifiers are accepted without warnings. **Strongest improvement** is selected, so the displayed prices do not affect the order. The displayed timing is one local run, not a performance guarantee. No item was purchased or equipped for these captures.

The PoE1 **Jewel planning example** is a synthetic development fixture, separate from Ghost Activity's guide. It searches Lethal Pride seeds at Arsenal of Vengeance (socket 2491), with total Strength weighted at 1 and scoring not restricted to allocated nodes. Its 8,001 matching seeds and scores come from PoB's finder; the score is not total build DPS. The fixture is not a recommended or complete character build.

## Reusing and refreshing captures

These files can be used in the widget README and marketplace gallery. Keep the matching captions and [project/source credits](../README.md#credits). Path of Exile artwork and the official website belong to Grinding Gear Games; the calculation and editing functionality comes from Path of Building Community. Do not describe these screenshots as original game artwork or imply endorsement.

For website captures, scroll the account/header area out of view before taking the screenshot. Inspect the final pixels for the signed-in username, private messages, account menus, browser history and unrelated desktop content. Do not commit a capture while verification or sign-in is showing. Capture settled comparisons/results and label historical prices as examples rather than purchase recommendations.

Refresh the images when the UI changes. Preserve actual values and labels; do not paint over controls, replace numbers or fabricate listings to make a screenshot look more favourable.
