WidgetRail WinUI controller font subsets

These generated subsets preserve the controller outlines from Kenney Input
Prompts (CC0 1.0) and PromptFont (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The Guide subset
remains under SIL OFL 1.1 and retains the original copyright notices:
Copyright 2018-2026, Yukari Hafner; Copyright 2011-2016, Severin Meyer.
Its modified font family is WidgetRail Controller Guide.
The Kenney subsets remain under CC0 1.0.
Their outlines are scaled and
centered within the font em to match WidgetRail's controller prompt display
contract (88 percent optical fit; 1.35-em shoulder/trigger cells).

Original source fonts, licenses and notices remain in ../kenney and ../promptfont.
The application distributes those licenses and notices alongside this notice.
No fonts are registered with Windows. WinUI loads the private packaged subsets.

Regenerate with scripts/Generate-WinUiControllerFonts.py and fonttools 4.66.0.
inventory.json records source/output hashes and the included glyph bounds.
The generator and Python are not application or ordinary build dependencies.
